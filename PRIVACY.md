# Privacy policy: Score Hider for MyAnimeList

Score Hider for MyAnimeList collects no personal information and no usage data.

- It runs only on pages of myanimelist.net, where it hides community scores, ranks and counts.
- It makes no network requests: nothing it handles is sent to the developer or to anyone else.
- It keeps two things in the browser's own extension storage, on your device: your settings, and,
  only if you choose the setting that remembers shows, the shows whose score you clicked. Removing
  the extension removes them; the settings page can also forget the remembered shows.
- It has no analytics, no advertising and no accounts.

Questions about this policy can be raised where the extension's listing gives a contact.

Last updated: 13 November 2026.
